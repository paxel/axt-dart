# Vectors

`shares.json` and `bricks.json` are `vectors/shares/shares.json` and
`vectors/bricks/bricks.json` of the AXT 0.2.0 release
(https://github.com/paxel/axt/releases/tag/v0.2.0), unchanged, under CC0-1.0 as all
vectors of AXT are. `test/proposals_test.dart` plays the owner and proposer sessions of
`shares.json`, and `test/bricks_test.dart` answers every vector of `bricks.json`; the
conformance job runs every vector of the release.
